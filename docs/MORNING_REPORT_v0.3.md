So the count is above chance level, but the interval is wide. Limit: 20 subjects, about 45 trials each; p-values are at the 1/201 floor for 2 subjects.
**Q2 ERD vs decoder score: yes, they match.** Mu-band LS (C3=8, C4=12, verified on the EDF names) is < 0 in 13 of 20 subjects. Spearman LS vs csp_lda within score: rho -0.6351, p 0.0026, n 20 (more negative LS goes with a higher score). This supports "non-responders are a physiology effect, not a pipeline defect". Limit: a correlation over 20 subjects; LS and the score come from the same trials.
**Q3 calibration by group: neither group shows a measurable difference.** Gain = source+target(k) - k=0 per target (csp_lda, 3 draws per k); responders minus non-responders, bootstrap 95% CI: k=4 -0.0075 [-0.0637, 0.0501], k=8 0.0129 [-0.0291, 0.0683], k=16 0.0388 [-0.0175, 0.1198], k=24 0.0277 [-0.0576, 0.0982]. Non-responder mean gain is between -0.0042 and 0.0016 at every k. Limits: 3 responders vs 17; responders were selected on a high score, so regression to the mean applies. For all 20 subjects, ts_lr source+target beats k=0 at k=4, +0.0258 [0.0032, 0.0520] (also at k=8 and k=16, not at k=24); csp_lda and fbcsp_lr never do.

**Lessons:**
1. Check that the other agent is alive at the first marker. A heartbeat with a timestamp would have shown at 04:30 that Codex was idle; 6 h of its time was lost.
2. Probe every new gate with a reference before locking. The G9 rank probe turned an impossible gate into a 1.225 s target.
3. Owner-scoped gates (fail on the owner branch, skip elsewhere) kept branch runs readable and still failed loudly on integration until the Codex code arrived.
4. Freeze the tree during G6, or keep dirty-state out of results.json: one doc edit made two identical runs "differ".
5. Size memory, not only time: 4 workers, each with a 4 GB cache cap, is more than this PC has once the data is 3x PhysioNet.
